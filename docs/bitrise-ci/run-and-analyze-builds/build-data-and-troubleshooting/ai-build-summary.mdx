Our AI analyzer gives you a summary of why a CI build failed and suggests the fix right there on the build page.

![Bitrise AI panel with Failure details, Reason, and Suggested solution](/img/run-and-analyze-builds/2026-07-14-bitrise-ai-failure-details-panel.png)

To enable the feature:

:::note[Disabling AI]

If you prefer not to use any AI features at all, you can disable all of them through the AI Settings menu in your <GlossTerm baseform="workspace">Workspace</GlossTerm> settings page: [Enabling AI features on Bitrise](/bitrise-platform/ai/enabling-ai-features-on-bitrise)

:::

1. <Partial_GettingToTheProjectSettingsPage />
1. Select **Bitrise AI** on the left.
1. Find **Summary** and toggle it on.


## Where the summary appears

The summary appears on the build page on the Bitrise website. You can also post it as a PR comment: in the **Repository** settings of your project, turn on PR comments and select the **Build summary** information block. For details, see [Reporting build problems in PR comments](/bitrise-ci/run-and-analyze-builds/build-data-and-troubleshooting/reporting-build-problems-in-pr-comments).

Once you turn on **Summary**, status indicators below the toggle show where the summary currently appears:

- **Bitrise website**: always active.
- **PR comments**: active when PR comments are enabled with the **Build summary** block selected.
