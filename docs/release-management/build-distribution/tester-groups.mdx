Create tester groups in Release Management to to be able to distribute installable artifacts to testers automatically. When a new installable artifact is available, the tester groups can be:

- Automatically notified via email.
- Manually notified via email.

The notification email contains a link to the build page in Release Management.

A connected app can have multiple tester groups. There are two types of tester groups:

- Internal groups are for members of your workspace, including members who hold a project or app role. Internal groups can be notified automatically or manually.
- External groups are for testers outside your workspace, identified by their email addresses. They don't need a Bitrise account. External groups are not notified automatically: you send them an access link for a build instead.

:::important[Required access level]

Users with the following roles can manage tester groups:

- <GlossTerm baseform="workspace">Workspace</GlossTerm> owner
- Project admin

:::

## Creating tester groups

The type of a group can't be changed after you create it.

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="internal" label="Internal group" default>

1. Open your connected app in Release Management.
1. Select **Build distribution** and then the **Tester groups** tab.
1. On the **Internal testing** tab, click **+ New group**.
1. Enter a group name.

   The name must be unique: no other tester group can have the same name. You can change the name later.
1. Optionally, check the **Send notifications automatically** box.

   With this setting, every group member receives a notification when a new installable artifact is available. You can change this later.
1. Click **Create group**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="external" label="External group">

1. Open your connected app in Release Management.
1. Select **Build distribution** and then the **Tester groups** tab.
1. On the **External testing** tab, click **+ New group**.
1. Enter a group name.

   The name must be unique: no other tester group can have the same name. You can change the name later.
1. In the **Testers** field, enter the testers' email addresses, separated by commas or spaces.

   You must enter at least one valid address.
1. Click **Create group**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Adding members to tester groups

A newly created internal tester group is empty. External groups start with the email addresses you entered when you created them. You can add any member of the workspace to an internal group, including members who hold a [project role](/bitrise-platform/projects/roles-and-permissions-for-bitrise-ci) or a role on the connected app. Members who don't have access to the connected app are flagged in the tester list.

To add members to a tester group:

1. Open your connected app in Release Management.
1. Select **Build distribution** and then the **Tester groups** tab.
1. Find the group and click the options menu (⋮).

   ![manage-tester-group.png](/img/_paligo/uuid-fd63cf77-e2f7-bfce-3000-4a80b87f158f.png)
1. Select **Manage testers**.
1. In the group page, click **Add testers**.
1. Select all the testers you need and click **Add**.

## Removing members from tester groups

You can remove members from testing groups in two ways:

- Removing them from a specific testing group.
- Removing them from all testing groups at the same time.

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="specific-group" label="Specific group" default>

1. Open your connected app in Release Management.
1. Select **Build distribution** and then the **Tester groups** tab.
1. Find the group and click the options menu (⋮).

   ![manage-tester-group.png](/img/_paligo/uuid-fd63cf77-e2f7-bfce-3000-4a80b87f158f.png)
1. Select **Manage testers**.
1. In the **Internal tester group members** list, find the tester you want to remove and click **Remove tester from group**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="all-groups" label="All groups">

1. Open your connected app in Release Management.
1. Select **Build distribution** and then the **Testers** tab.
1. On the **Internal testers** tab, find the tester you want to remove and click **Remove from all tester groups**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Configuring notification settings for tester groups

Tester groups can be notified automatically via email: whenever a new installable artifact is available for the app, each member of a tester group gets an email notification.

The notification email contains a link to the build page in Release Management, from where you can install the app on a mobile device.

:::note[New members]

You can only send a notification once. If you add new members to the group after a notification email has been sent, those new members won't get a notification email.

:::

To configure automatic notifications:

1. Open your connected app in Release Management.
1. Select **Build distribution** and then the **Tester groups** tab.
1. Find the group and click the options menu (⋮).
1. Select **Configure notifications**.
1. Toggle on **Automatic notifications**.

## Sending notifications to tester groups manually

You can notify a tester group manually about a new installable build artifact. Internal groups get a notification email. For external groups, you create and send an access link instead.

:::note[New members]

You can only send a notification once. If you add new members to the group after a notification email has been sent, those new members won't get a notification email.

:::

1. Open your connected app in Release Management.
1. Select **Build distribution** and then the **Builds** tab.
1. Find the build you need from the list and click the arrow on the right.
1. On the build page, select the **Testing** tab.

   ![tester-settings-page.png](/img/_paligo/uuid-c8b3be87-8669-d4d7-d100-ad03c3055645.png)
1. Find the tester group and click **Send**.

### Sending an access link to an external tester group

External groups are not notified automatically. To give them access to a build, send them an access link:

1. Open your connected app in Release Management.
1. Select **Build distribution** and then the **Builds** tab.
1. Find the build you need from the list and click the arrow on the right.
1. On the build page, select the **Testing** tab.
1. In the external testing table, find the tester group and click **Create & send**.
1. Optionally, set an access code or an expiration for the link, then click **Create & send**.

Once the link has been sent, the group's row shows **Sent**.
