All apps in Release Management have a title and app icon. You can edit the title and use your own custom icon.

:::important[App icon requirements]

The supported formats for the app icon are JPEG and PNG. It must have a non-transparent background. The maximum file size is 1 MB.

:::

1. <Partial_OpeningAnAppInReleaseManagement />
1. On the left, select **Basic settings**. Note that only **Project Admins** and **<GlossTerm baseform="workspace">Workspace</GlossTerm> Owners** can access the **Basic settings** menu for an application.
1. In the **Appearance** section, you can:

   - Click **Edit title** to change the title. Set the new title in the dialog and then click **Save**.
   - Click **Change icon** to change the app icon. In the dialog, drag-and-drop a new icon. It must be a JPEG or a PNG with a non-transparent background, no bigger than 1 MB.

:::note[App icons and our API]

If you prefer, you can also change the app icon using our [API](https://api.bitrise.io/release-management/api-docs/index.html#/Connected%20Apps%20-%20Public%20Assets/GeneratePublicAssetUploadUrl).

:::
