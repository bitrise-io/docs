:::tip[Work on your configuration locally]

The [offline Workflow Editor](/bitrise-ci/bitrise-cli/installing-and-upgrading-the-offline-workflow-editor) runs on your own computer: you can edit your app's `bitrise.yml` file without logging in to [bitrise.io](https://www.bitrise.io/), and your configuration never leaves your network. It offers most features of the Workflow Editor.

:::
