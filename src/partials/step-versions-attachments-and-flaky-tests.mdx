:::important[Attachments and flaky tests]

If your tests generate attachments or you want Bitrise to show flaky tests, the **Deploy to Bitrise.io** Step must be of version 2.19.1 or newer. To link attachments to test cases by filename, it must be version 2.26.0 or newer: [Naming attachments after their test case](/bitrise-ci/testing/test-reports/test-attachments/naming-attachments-after-test-cases).

:::
