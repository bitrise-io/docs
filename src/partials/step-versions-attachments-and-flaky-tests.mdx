:::important[Attachments and flaky tests]

To show flaky tests, the **Deploy to Bitrise.io** Step must be version 2.17.0 or newer. To show attachments, it must be version 2.19.0 or newer, or 2.21.0 or newer for video attachments. To link attachments to test cases by filename, it must be version 2.26.0 or newer: [Naming attachments after their test case](/bitrise-ci/testing/test-reports/test-attachments/naming-attachments-after-test-cases).

:::
